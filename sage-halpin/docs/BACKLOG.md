# Backlog: proposal capabilities Sixonic does not have

The rebrand keeps Sixonic's functionality and does not build these. They come
from the Sage Halpin proposal and are recorded here so the rebrand does not turn
into a rewrite.

- **Shared, durable workspace.** Today everything lives in one browser. A
  client-owned store (Azure Database for PostgreSQL, row-level security per
  organisation) would make decisions, actions and outcomes shared and durable.
- **Sign-in and roles.** Microsoft Entra ID / External ID, with decision rights
  reserved for authorised people.
- **Human advisers on the team.** Today the people are the CEO and whoever they
  share the screen with. The proposal adds named human advisers who join and
  leave with a remit and a handover.
- **Team timeline.** How the team scaled and flexed over time, and why.
- **Institutional memory.** Provenance, versioning, contradiction and
  stale-knowledge review, retention and deletion.
- **Handover** when an adviser leaves, traceable to sources.
- **Decision room.** Evidence map (fact / interpretation / recommendation),
  options, dissent, and a decision recorded only by an authorised person.
- **Bespoke challenges.** Sixonic already promised these as "coming soon".
- **Engagements.** First Decision, Living Board, Group Intelligence.
- **Audit trail**, hash-chained per organisation, once there is shared state to
  audit.
