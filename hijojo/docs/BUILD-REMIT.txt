Hijojo Agentic Prospecting — Refined Build Remit

Purpose

Build one tightly bounded agentic capability inside Hijojo.

Its sole purpose is to take an AIGoGo OpCo, understand what it sells and who should buy it, identify a small number of highly qualified prospects, conduct individually relevant outreach, follow up once after five days, and route positive responses to mark@aigogo.ai.

This is not a generic bulk lead-generation or email-blast platform.

Quality, relevance and qualification take priority over volume.

⸻

1. USER ENTERS OPCO

User enters or selects an AIGoGo OpCo.

Minimum input:

* OpCo name
* website
* optional supporting documents/information.

The platform should then perform the research itself.

Do not require the user to manually create prospecting criteria where these can be reliably established from the OpCo and available evidence.

⸻

2. UNDERSTAND THE OPCO

The platform autonomously evaluates the OpCo.

Analyse:

* proposition
* products/services
* problem solved
* target customer
* likely buyer
* business benefit
* USP
* differentiation
* commercial model
* indicative customer cost
* implementation requirements
* proof/evidence available
* strongest sales arguments
* weaknesses or unsupported claims.

The platform must distinguish between:

FACT

Supported by evidence.

INFERENCE

Reasonably inferred.

UNKNOWN

Requires confirmation.

Unsupported marketing claims must not be invented to improve outreach.

⸻

3. CREATE PROSPECTING PROFILE

Convert the analysis into a structured Prospecting Profile.

Include:

Proposition

What exactly are we selling?

Problem

What identifiable problem does it solve?

ICP

Define tightly:

* sector
* subsector
* geography
* company size
* revenue where relevant
* employee range
* technology/environment
* maturity
* ownership
* growth profile
* regulatory characteristics
* other relevant attributes.

Buyer

Identify likely:

* economic buyer
* operational sponsor
* technical stakeholder
* influencer.

Buying Signals

Define observable conditions indicating a genuine reason to buy.

Examples could include:

* recruitment
* expansion
* acquisition
* new leadership
* transformation programme
* technology change
* supplier change
* contract expiry
* regulatory pressure
* cost pressure
* funding
* poor performance
* new strategic initiative.

Signals must be specific to the OpCo proposition.

Exclusions

Explicitly identify companies that should not be approached.

⸻

4. PROSPECT QUALIFICATION

The platform searches for potential prospects.

Do not create huge generic lists.

Each target must pass a qualification test.

Score:

ICP Fit — 30

Current Need / Trigger — 30

Buyer Fit — 15

Timing — 10

Commercial Potential — 10

Evidence Quality — 5

Total:

100

Default outreach threshold:

80/100

A prospect below threshold should not automatically receive outreach.

The system should prefer:

20 excellent prospects over 2,000 mediocre prospects.

⸻

5. PROSPECT EVIDENCE

Every prospect record must explain:

Why this company?

Specific evidence.

Why now?

Specific trigger or current reason.

Why this proposition?

Connection between identified problem and the OpCo capability.

Who?

Best identifiable relevant decision-maker.

Evidence

Sources supporting the qualification.

Confidence

0–100%.

No prospect should be contacted merely because:

“They are a company in the target sector.”

⸻

6. PRE-CONTACT QA

Before outreach, run an independent QA pass.

Check:

* company identity
* decision-maker relevance
* proposition relevance
* evidence freshness
* claims
* personalisation
* duplicate contact
* exclusion status
* previous response
* suppression status
* communication appropriateness.

If QA fails:

DO NOT SEND.

Return prospect for research or discard.

⸻

7. INTRODUCTION

For qualified prospects, create an individually relevant introduction.

The content must reflect:

THE PROSPECT

not merely the OpCo.

Structure:

Why them → why now → relevant problem → short proposition → teaser → link

Do not produce generic AI-generated sales copy.

Bad:

We are an innovative AI company helping organisations transform…

Good communication should demonstrate that there is a specific, evidenced reason the recipient was selected.

⸻

8. INTRO LINK + TEASER

First contact contains:

* concise introduction
* relevant observation
* proposition teaser
* introduction/demo link
* clear low-friction CTA.

Keep it short.

Do not explain the whole product.

Create enough relevance and curiosity for the prospect to investigate.

All links must be checked immediately before sending.

⸻

9. FIVE-DAY FOLLOW-UP

If there is no response after five days, send one follow-up.

The follow-up must not simply say:

Just following up on my previous email.

It should add relevant value.

Include:

* short contextual reminder
* another relevant observation or benefit
* short teaser
* website/product link
* simple CTA.

Where appropriate, refresh prospect intelligence before generating the follow-up so new information can improve relevance.

⸻

10. RESPONSE HANDLING

Any prospect response immediately stops automated follow-up.

Classify response:

* Positive
* Interested / question
* Referral
* Not now
* Not interested
* Unsubscribe
* Other.

For Positive, Interested / question, or a potentially useful Referral:

Send to mark@aigogo.ai

Include:

* OpCo
* prospect company
* prospect name
* role
* contact details available
* original outreach
* response
* qualification score
* reason selected
* supporting evidence
* relevant links
* recommended next action.

Do not allow the prospect to continue through an autonomous sales sequence after engagement.

A human takes over.

⸻

11. NO-RESPONSE RULE

The automated sequence is deliberately constrained:

Qualified

↓

Introduction

↓

Wait 5 days

↓

No response

↓

One relevant follow-up

↓

Stop

Do not create uncontrolled sequences of automated messages.

⸻

12. RELEVANCE ENGINE — NON-NEGOTIABLE

Every communication must pass a relevance test.

Before sending, the system must be able to answer:

1. Why this company?
2. Why this person?
3. Why now?
4. Why this OpCo?
5. What evidence supports the message?
6. Is the content materially specific to this recipient?

If it cannot answer all six satisfactorily:

DO NOT SEND.

⸻

13. TDD — NON-NEGOTIABLE

Build using Test-Driven Development.

Tests are written before implementation for every material function.

Minimum test suites:

OpCo Analysis

Test correct extraction of:

* proposition
* ICP
* USP
* cost
* buyer
* buying signals.

Prospect Discovery

Test:

* ICP matching
* exclusions
* evidence collection
* scoring
* threshold enforcement.

Communications

Test:

* prospect-specific relevance
* unsupported claims
* correct links
* correct OpCo
* correct recipient
* appropriate CTA.

Sequence

Test:

Qualified → Send → Wait 5 days → Check response → Follow-up OR stop.

Response

Test:

Response received → automation stops → classify → qualifying response sent to mark@aigogo.ai.

Safety

Test:

* duplicate prevention
* unsubscribe
* suppression
* bounced address
* invalid recipient
* previous response
* failed link
* missing evidence
* low qualification
* conflicting prospect data.

Every defect found must result in a regression test before the defect is considered fixed.

⸻

14. END-TO-END TESTING — NON-NEGOTIABLE

Test the complete journey rather than isolated components.

Create synthetic test OpCos and prospects.

Required E2E scenarios:

Scenario A

Excellent prospect → qualified → introduction → responds → Mark receives handoff.

Scenario B

Excellent prospect → introduction → no response → five-day follow-up → stop.

Scenario C

Poor prospect → score below threshold → never contacted.

Scenario D

Strong company but wrong person → QA fails → never contacted.

Scenario E

Prospect replies before day five → follow-up cancelled.

Scenario F

Prospect unsubscribes → immediately suppressed.

Scenario G

Agent creates unsupported claim → QA rejects communication.

Scenario H

Broken/incorrect link → send prevented.

Scenario I

Duplicate prospect → duplicate communication prevented.

Scenario J

Positive response → complete prospect intelligence and conversation forwarded to Mark.

⸻

15. SELF-QA — NON-NEGOTIABLE

Introduce an independent QA agent/process.

The agent generating a prospect or communication must not be solely responsible for approving its own output.

Use:

Research Agent

↓

Qualification Agent

↓

Communication Agent

↓

Independent QA

↓

Send

The QA stage can:

PASS

REWORK

REJECT

Only PASS permits external communication.

⸻

16. CONTINUOUS QA

Monitor:

* qualification accuracy
* emails sent
* delivery
* opens/clicks where legitimately available
* replies
* positive replies
* negative replies
* unsubscribe rate
* false-positive prospects
* QA rejection rate
* response by ICP
* response by trigger
* response by message approach.

Use outcomes to improve qualification.

Do not allow the system to weaken qualification simply to increase sending volume.

18. AGENTIC BOUNDARY

The platform is autonomous only within this defined workflow.

It may autonomously:

* research the OpCo
* construct the ICP
* identify prospects
* research prospects
* score prospects
* reject weak prospects
* identify contacts
* prepare communications
* QA communications
* send approved introductions
* detect responses
* send the five-day follow-up
* classify responses
* hand qualified responses to Mark.

It must not autonomously:

* negotiate
* quote bespoke pricing
* make contractual commitments
* change commercial terms
* make unsupported claims
* continue selling after a response
* create arbitrary additional follow-ups.

⸻

19. SUCCESS MEASURES

Do not judge success primarily by number of prospects or messages.

Primary metrics:

Qualified Prospect Rate

How many discovered organisations genuinely satisfy the ICP?

Contact Accuracy

Did we reach the correct person?

Relevance

Would a reasonable recipient understand specifically why they were contacted?

Positive Response Rate

How many qualified contacts generated useful engagement?

Human-Accepted Opportunity Rate

How many handoffs does Mark consider genuinely worth pursuing?

False Positive Rate

How many prospects should never have been contacted?

This should continually decrease.

Non-Negotiable Acceptance Standard

The capability is not production-ready merely because it can find companies and send emails.

It is production-ready only when end-to-end testing demonstrates that it can repeatedly:

understand an AIGoGo OpCo, identify a genuinely relevant company, establish an evidenced reason why that company should care now, identify an appropriate individual, generate communication specifically relevant to that prospect, independently QA it, send it, follow up correctly, recognise a response, stop automation and reliably hand the opportunity to mark@aigogo.ai.

Precision over volume.

Evidence over assumption.

Relevance over generic personalisation.

Qualified conversations over email activity.
